# Content and rights checklist
- Verify important claims with primary sources and independent corroboration.
- Keep asset provider, original URL, creator and license. Do not use unclear-license assets.
- No scraped creator videos or unlicensed music.
- Sponsored content, brand assets and product claims need substantiation, approval and disclosure.
- Human review is required before public publishing.
- Avoid automated medical, financial, legal, emergency or safety advice.
- Follow platform community, monetization and API policies.
